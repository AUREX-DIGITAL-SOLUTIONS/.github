# Aurex Digital Solutions

<img src="./assets/Aurex-transparent.png" alt="Aurex logo" width="220" />

Aurex Digital Solutions is building open digital energy coordination infrastructure for African energy systems, starting from Kenya.

## Focus

- Open and interoperable energy coordination infrastructure
- Public-interest, security, and institutional governance
- Research-led validation before deployment claims

## Current Status

Aurex is under institutional development. This work is a documentation and research baseline, not a claim of regulatory authority, utility operation, or national platform deployment.
