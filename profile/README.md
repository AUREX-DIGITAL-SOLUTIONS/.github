# Aurex Digital Solutions

**Open, interoperable digital infrastructure for Africa's energy transition.**

Aurex Digital Solutions is a public-interest, open-source institution researching, developing, validating, and stewarding the digital operating layer that helps African energy systems become more reliable, resilient, interoperable, secure, affordable, and transparent.

Kenya-origin. Africa-oriented.

---

## Why Aurex exists

Africa has exceptional renewable-energy potential — solar, wind, geothermal, hydro, and bioenergy — yet many power systems remain constrained by fragmented data, limited real-time visibility, weak coordination between distributed assets, and under-digitized operating architecture.

Adding generation capacity is necessary, but not sufficient. Without a digital operating layer, new renewable assets, batteries, mini-grids, facilities, and flexible loads remain under-coordinated and under-utilized.

Aurex exists to help build that digital layer — openly, transparently, and in the public interest.

## What we do

- **Research** — real energy-system constraints, user needs, and technical requirements.
- **Develop** — open, modular, interoperable software, data models, and reference architectures.
- **Validate** — through prototypes, facility-level pilots, and evidence-based iteration.
- **Steward** — quality, governance, security posture, and long-term public-interest alignment.
- **Coordinate** — with institutions, utilities, regulators, developers, and communities.

## What we are not

Aurex Digital Solutions is not a government agency, licensed utility, regulator, system operator, or national grid operator. We build research, tools, standards, prototypes, and pilots — we do not claim authority that belongs to public institutions or licensed market participants.
